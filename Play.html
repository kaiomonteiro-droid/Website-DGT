Why play RPGs

The commonly races in a medieval RPGs

Pick Your Game: Tabletop RPGs like Dungeons & Dragons use dice, character sheets, and a guide (the Game Master). Video RPGs are digital games with quests and character progression. Live-action RPGs (LARP) involve acting out your character in real life with costumes and props.

Make a Character: Decide who your character is (personality, goals, and backstory). Choose special skills, powers, or abilities your character has. Pick starting equipment or items for your character.

Learn Basic Rules: Know how actions and dice rolls work to decide outcomes. Understand teamwork and what your character can do, but don’t worry about memorizing everything at once.

Play the Game: Make decisions as your character would. Work with other players to solve problems or complete quests. Roll dice (or follow the game mechanics) to see what happens.

Have Fun and Explore: Help tell the story together, explore the world, and face challenges. Learn from mistakes and enjoy creative problem-solving.

Quick Tips: Begin with simple games or pre-made adventures. Focus on having fun instead of winning. Ask questions if you are unsure about rules; you’ll learn as you play.
